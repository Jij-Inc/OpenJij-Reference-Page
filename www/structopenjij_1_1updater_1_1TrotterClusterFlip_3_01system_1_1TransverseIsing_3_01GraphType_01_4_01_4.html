Detailed Description

template<typename GraphType>
struct openjij::updater::TrotterClusterFlip< system::TransverseIsing< GraphType > >

Trotter-direction cluster flip for the discrete-time transverse field Ising model.

For every classical spin \(i\) the ring of its \(M\) Trotter copies is an Ising chain with ferromagnetic coupling \(J_\perp = -\frac12\ln\tanh(\beta\Gamma(1-s)/M)\). Neighbouring equal copies are bonded with probability \(1-e^{-2J_\perp}\) (Swendsen-Wang on the chain), and every resulting segment is flipped with Metropolis probability \(\min(1, e^{-s\beta\Delta E/M})\), where \(\Delta E\) is the classical energy change summed over the segment.

  • \(J_\perp\to 0\): every segment is one copy, i.e. the move reduces to single spin flip Metropolis.
  • \(J_\perp\to\infty\): the whole ring flips at once (a "global move"), so the replicas keep moving even when they are locked together.

The bond probability \(e^{-2J_\perp}=\tanh(\beta\Gamma(1-s)/M)\) is evaluated directly, so \(s=1\) is well defined.
